# Format and workflow (draft v0.1)

## Three cadences, three products

Each cadence gets its own subscription list, so people can opt into only the weekly edition.

| | **Daily Pulse** | **Weekly Radar** (flagship) | **Monthly Outlook** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | "Did anything material happen?" | The main trend read for product decisions | A strategic synthesis for leadership and the board |
| Length | 3–5 bullets, about a 1-minute read | About a 5-minute read | About a 10-minute read |
| Send rule | **Only when there's something material.** No news means no email | Every week | First week of each month |
| Your review | Optional; auto-draft only | **Brainstorm session before send** | **Brainstorm session before send** |
| Sections | Headlines only, with links | All sections below | Top 5 themes, trend score movement, funding totals by region, roadmap implications |

## Weekly Radar: section order

1. **Header:** Immix Protect logo, "Trend Radar", edition number, date range
2. **From the VP of Product Transformation:** your manual highlights, written in the brainstorm session (2–4 sentences)
3. **The 3 signals that matter this week:** a TL;DR, one line each
4. **Priority lane: video and virtual monitoring**, grouped by region: 🇺🇸 US → 🇬🇧 UK → 🌍 EMEA → 🇿🇦 South Africa → (other regions only if material)
5. **Startup and funding radar:** a table of new accelerator graduates (YC, Techstars and others) and large rounds. Columns: company, region, programme or round, what they do, relevance
6. **Regulation and standards:** what changed and what it means for the product
7. **What this means for the roadmap:** 2–3 questions or bets for product and R&D to discuss
8. **General security:** short bullets, deliberately at the bottom
9. **Footer:** sources, feedback link, unsubscribe

## Each trend item has

- **Headline**, plus a **2-sentence summary** of what happened
- **Why it matters for Immix:** one line tying it to integrations, monitoring-centre workflow, false-alarm reduction, verified response, and so on
- **Signal tag:** `ACT` (needs a roadmap or commercial response), `WATCH` (track it, revisit next month) or `EXPLORE` (worth a conversation or partnership)
- **Type tag:** Funding · Launch · M&A · Regulation · Research · Partnership
- **Source link(s).** Every claim is linked, with no unsourced items

## Inclusion rules (proposed; please confirm the thresholds)

- **Funding:** accelerator graduates (YC, Techstars and peers) at any stage; otherwise Seed of **≥ $5M** or Series A and later of **≥ $15M**
- **Relevance score 1–5:** 5 = directly in monitoring-centre or video-verification workflow, 3 = adjacent (access control, IoT, public safety), 1 = general security. Items scoring ≤ 2 go only in the General security section
- **Other regions** (APAC, LATAM, Middle East outside EMEA coverage) appear only at relevance ≥ 4
- No item older than the cadence window, except in the monthly Outlook

## Review-then-send workflow

```
Thu         Claude researches and drafts the Weekly Radar (HTML + a shared review doc)
Thu/Fri     Brainstorm session with you: pick highlights, cut or reorder, write the "From the VP" note
Fri         Claude applies your edits and creates a Gmail draft to the mailing list (BCC)
Mon 08:00   You hit Send, or approve and Claude sends. The default is that you send.
```

The daily Pulse is drafted each morning as a Gmail draft. You can send it, skip it or ignore it. The monthly Outlook follows the weekly flow in the last week of the month.
