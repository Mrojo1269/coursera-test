# Format and workflow (draft v0.2)

**Audience:** internal only. Each email carries an "Internal: do not forward" line.
**Sender:** drafts are created in Melissa Rojo's Gmail (Melissa.rojo@immixprotect.com) and sent by Melissa.

## Three cadences, three products

Each cadence gets its own subscription list, so people can opt into only the weekly edition.

| | **Daily Pulse** | **Weekly Radar** (flagship) | **Monthly Outlook** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | "Did anything material happen?" | The main trend read for product decisions | A strategic synthesis for leadership |
| Length | 3–5 bullets, about a 1-minute read | About a 5-minute read | About a 10-minute read |
| Send rule | **Only when there's something material.** No news means no email | Every week | First week of each month |
| Your review | Gmail draft; send, skip or edit | **Brainstorm session before send** | **Brainstorm session before send** |
| Sections | Headlines only, with links | All sections below | Top 5 themes, trend movement, funding totals by region, legislation outlook, roadmap implications |

## Weekly Radar: section order

1. **Header:** Immix Protect branding, "Trend Radar", edition number, date range
2. **From the VP of Product Transformation:** your manual highlights, written in the brainstorm session
3. **The 3 signals that matter this week:** a TL;DR
4. **Priority lane: video and virtual monitoring**, grouped by region: 🇺🇸 US → 🇬🇧 UK → 🌍 EMEA → 🇿🇦 South Africa → (other regions only if material)
5. **Ecosystem updates:** key updates from established players, including partners and integrations (releases, partnerships, M&A, leadership, pricing)
6. **Companies to watch:** emerging companies, plus **every funding round in the space at any size** and accelerator graduates (YC, Techstars and others)
7. **Legislation, policy and talks:** enacted laws, proposed bills, consultations, hearings, and notable conference talks by regulators, police and industry bodies, each with its likely product impact
8. **What this means for the roadmap:** 2–3 questions or bets for product and R&D
9. **General security:** short bullets, deliberately at the bottom
10. **Footer:** internal-only notice, signal key, feedback

Section names never label a company by relationship. "Ecosystem updates" covers partners and other established players alike.

## Each trend item has

- **Headline**, plus a **2-sentence summary** of what happened
- **Why it matters for Immix:** one line tying it to integrations, monitoring-centre workflow, false-alarm reduction, verified response, and so on
- **Signal tag:** `ACT` (needs a roadmap or commercial response), `WATCH` (track it) or `EXPLORE` (worth a conversation or partnership)
- **Type tag:** Funding · Launch · M&A · Legislation · Policy talk · Research · Partnership
- **Source link(s).** Every claim is linked, with no unsourced items

## Inclusion rules

- **Funding:** report **all** rounds and accelerator graduates in the space, at any size. The rest of the security space gets a one-line mention in General security when notable
- **Legislation:** include it at any stage, from talk or proposal through consultation and enactment, as long as it touches video, monitoring, AI analytics, biometrics, alarm response, private security or connected-device security
- **Relevance score 1–5:** 5 = directly in monitoring-centre or video-verification workflow, 3 = adjacent (access control, IoT, public safety), 1 = general security. Items scoring ≤ 2 go in General security
- **Other regions** (APAC, LATAM and others) appear only at relevance ≥ 4

## Review-then-send workflow

```
Thu         Claude researches and drafts the Weekly Radar
Thu/Fri     Brainstorm session with you: pick highlights, cut or reorder, write the "From the VP" note
Fri         Claude applies your edits and creates the Gmail draft (mailing list in BCC)
Mon 08:00   You review the draft in Gmail and hit Send
```

The daily Pulse lands as a Gmail draft each morning when there's news. The monthly Outlook follows the weekly flow in the last week of the month.
