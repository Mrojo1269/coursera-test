# Sources (draft v0.1)

Every source is tagged with:
- **Lane:** `VM` (video, virtual and remote monitoring: the priority lane), `FUND` (startups, accelerators and funding), `REG` (regulation and standards) or `GEN` (general security and cyber, which goes at the bottom of the email).
- **Region:** US, UK, EMEA, ZA (South Africa) or GLOBAL.
- **Access:** free, or 💳 for a paid subscription. Please confirm which paid ones we already have.

## Tier 3: legislation, policy and talks (product constraints and opportunities)

This tier covers **enacted laws**, **proposed bills and consultations**, **hearings**, and **talks** (keynotes, panels and speeches by regulators, police and industry bodies) that point toward a coming change.

| Source | Region | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Congress.gov, FCC "Covered List" / NDAA §889, CISA | US | Camera and hardware vendor restrictions, IoT security rules |
| State legislatures and city councils (via LegiScan or news), police department policy updates | US | Verified-response and alarm-dispatch ordinances, biometric/privacy laws (IL BIPA, CO, TX), AI-in-surveillance bills |
| UL 827 / 827B, TMA-AVS-01, TMA Annual Meeting and TMA webinars, SIA GovSummit | US | Monitoring centre standards; where policy debates are heading |
| UK Parliament bills tracker, Home Office and DSIT consultations, ICO, Biometrics and Surveillance Camera Commissioner, NPCC alarm policy | UK | Surveillance codes, AI regulation, police response policy |
| BS 8418, BS EN 50518, BSIA and NSI briefings | UK/EMEA | Remote video response centre and alarm-receiving-centre (ARC) standards |
| EU AI Act implementation (AI Office guidance, high-risk and remote-biometric provisions), EDPB, NIS2, Cyber Resilience Act | EMEA | AI analytics compliance, connected-device security |
| Parliamentary Monitoring Group (pmg.org.za), PSIRA, Information Regulator (POPIA), SAIDSA | ZA | Private Security Industry Regulation Amendment Act, guarding and monitoring rules, data protection |
| Conference talks: ISC West, GSX, IFSEC, Securex SA, Intersec, RSAC (session recordings and recaps); security podcasts and webinars | GLOBAL | Early signals from regulators, police and leaders before rules are formalised |

## Tier 4: general security and cyber (goes at the bottom)

SecurityWeek, Dark Reading, The Record, CyberScoop, Krebs on Security, CISA advisories (especially ICS and IoT camera CVEs), Momentum Cyber and Pinpoint Search Group quarterly funding reports.

## Tier 5: early-signal sources (optional, noisier)

- Product Hunt and Hacker News "Show HN": security and computer-vision launches
- GitHub trending (computer vision, video analytics, edge AI)
- arXiv cs.CV: only for breakthroughs in edge video understanding and VLMs on camera feeds
- Hiring signals: tracked companies and startups posting for "monitoring centre", "remote guarding" and "VLM" roles
- Patent filings (USPTO, EPO) from tracked companies, monthly only
