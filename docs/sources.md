# Sources (draft v0.1)

Every source is tagged with:
- **Lane:** `VM` (video, virtual and remote monitoring: the priority lane), `FUND` (startups, accelerators and funding), `REG` (regulation and standards) or `GEN` (general security and cyber, which goes at the bottom of the email).
- **Region:** US, UK, EMEA, ZA (South Africa) or GLOBAL.
- **Access:** free, or 💳 for a paid subscription. Please confirm which paid ones we already have.

## Tier 3: regulation and standards (product constraints and opportunities)

| Source | Region | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| FCC "Covered List" / NDAA §889 updates | US | Restrictions on camera and hardware vendors |
| State biometric/privacy laws (IL BIPA, CO, TX), police verified-response policies | US | Analytics, facial recognition and alarm dispatch rules |
| UL 827 / 827B, TMA-AVS-01, CSAA/TMA standards | US | Monitoring centre certification |
| ICO, Biometrics and Surveillance Camera Commissioner, BS 8418, BS EN 50518 | UK | Remote video response centres, surveillance codes |
| EU AI Act (high-risk and remote-biometric provisions), GDPR/EDPB guidance, NIS2, Cyber Resilience Act | EMEA | AI analytics compliance, connected-device security |
| PSIRA, POPIA / Information Regulator | ZA | Private security regulation, data protection |

## Tier 4: general security and cyber (goes at the bottom)

SecurityWeek, Dark Reading, The Record, CyberScoop, Krebs on Security, CISA advisories (especially ICS and IoT camera CVEs), Momentum Cyber and Pinpoint Search Group quarterly funding reports.

## Tier 5: early-signal sources (optional, noisier)

- Product Hunt and Hacker News "Show HN": security and computer-vision launches
- GitHub trending (computer vision, video analytics, edge AI)
- arXiv cs.CV: only for breakthroughs in edge video understanding and VLMs on camera feeds
- Hiring signals: competitors and startups posting for "monitoring centre", "remote guarding" and "VLM" roles
- Patent filings (USPTO, EPO) from key competitors, monthly only
